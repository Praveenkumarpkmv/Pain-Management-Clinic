The Patient Experience

Care That Centers
Around You.

We understand that navigating medical care can be overwhelming, especially when you are already dealing with the burden of chronic pain. Our dedicated patient care team is here to ensure your journey is smooth, transparent, and as comfortable as possible. From your initial phone call to your final follow-up, we prioritize communication and compassion above all else.

Step by Step

What to Expect on Your First Visit

From the moment you walk through our doors, our team is dedicated to making your experience as comfortable and informative as possible. We believe in complete transparency, so you know exactly what is happening at every stage of your appointment.

01

Warm Welcome

Check in with our friendly front desk staff who will guide you through any remaining paperwork and answer initial questions. If you prefer, you can complete most of your intake forms online before you arrive to save time.

02

Clinical Assessment

A registered nurse will check your vitals, review your medical history, and ensure you are comfortable before seeing the doctor. This is also a great time to ask any logistical questions about your upcoming treatment or insurance coverage.

03

Physician Consultation

An in-depth, unhurried conversation with your pain specialist. We listen carefully to your story, review any prior imaging, and perform a focused physical and neurological exam to identify the exact source of your discomfort.

04

Clear Action Plan

Before you leave, we ensure you fully understand your diagnosis, treatment options, and next steps. We provide printed educational materials, schedule any follow-up procedures, and send a summary directly to your primary care physician.

Preparation is Key

Your Procedure Guide

Being prepared helps ensure your procedure is safe and successful. Following these guidelines will minimize the risk of complications and help you recover faster. If you have any specific medical conditions that might affect these instructions, please inform our team immediately.

Before Your Procedure

  • Medications: You may be asked to stop blood thinners (like aspirin, warfarin, or Plavix) several days prior to your procedure to prevent excessive bleeding. Always consult with our clinical team before making any changes to your medication regimen.
  • Fasting: Unless you are receiving IV sedation, you can usually eat a light meal before your procedure. If sedation is used, you must fast for at least 6 hours prior to your arrival time. Clear liquids are generally permitted up to 2 hours before.
  • Clothing: Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy access to the treatment area. Avoid wearing jewelry or tight undergarments that may interfere with the procedure.
  • Transportation: If you receive sedation, you must arrange for a responsible adult to drive you home and stay with you for the first 24 hours. You will not be permitted to drive yourself or take public transportation.

After Your Procedure

  • Rest: Take it easy for the first 24 hours following your procedure. Avoid strenuous activity, heavy lifting, or prolonged standing. Gentle walking is encouraged to prevent stiffness.
  • Ice/Heat: Apply ice to the injection site for 15 minutes at a time, several times a day, to reduce swelling and discomfort. Do not apply heat directly to the injection site for the first 48 hours.
  • Medications: Resume your normal medications unless instructed otherwise. Do not start any new pain medications without consulting our team. If you experience severe pain, contact our office immediately.
  • Follow-up: Keep your scheduled follow-up appointment. Relief may take a few days to fully take effect as the medication and local anesthetic wear off. Our team will monitor your progress and adjust your plan as needed.

Patient Rights & Responsibilities

At PainRelief, we are committed to fostering a culture of transparency, dignity, and mutual respect. Understanding your rights and responsibilities helps us provide the best possible care.

Your Rights

  • To be treated with dignity, respect, and consideration without discrimination.
  • To have your questions answered clearly and to participate in decisions about your care.
  • To confidentiality of your medical records and personal health information (HIPAA).
  • To receive information about your diagnosis, treatment options, and potential risks.
  • To request a copy of your medical records in a timely manner.

Your Responsibilities

  • To provide accurate and complete medical history and current medication lists.
  • To follow the agreed-upon treatment plan and notify us of any changes in your health.
  • To arrive on time for appointments and notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel.
  • To treat our physicians, nurses, and administrative staff with respect and courtesy.
  • To meet your financial obligations, including co-pays and deductibles, as agreed upon.
