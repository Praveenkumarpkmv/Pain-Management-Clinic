Patient & Doctor Resources

Appointment Readiness & Referrals

Whether you are visiting our clinic for the first time or a physician referring a patient with acute pain, we make coordination effortless and fast.

Patient Readiness

First-Visit Preparation Checklist

What to bring to ensure your evaluation is complete on day one

Diagnostic Imaging Discs & Reports

CD/DVD or USB of MRI, CT, or X-rays performed in the last 24 months, plus written radiologist findings.

Current Medication & Pain Log

Complete medication list with exact dosages, past injection dates (epidurals/facet blocks), and prior responses.

Insurance Cards & Photo ID

Primary and secondary coverage cards, photo identification, and physician referral letter (if required by HMO).

Comfortable Two-Piece Attire

Loose-fitting clothing (e.g. sweatpants or shorts) enabling focused physical examination of the spine and joints.

Contact FAQs

Quick answers to common inquiries.

We strive to schedule new patients within 3-5 business days. For urgent pain flare-ups, we often have same-week availability. Please call our scheduling line directly for the fastest service.
We are in-network with Medicare and most major commercial insurers (BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare). Please use the insurance verification tool on our Patient Care page or call our billing department to confirm your specific plan.
We kindly request at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling to avoid a cancellation fee. You can manage your appointments through our patient portal or by calling our front desk.
