Comprehensive Pain Care

Two Pathways to Lasting Relief

Whether you need targeted interventional procedures or a holistic medication management plan, our specialists tailor every treatment to your unique pain profile.

Nerve Blocks & Targeted Injections

Ultrasound and fluoroscopy-guided procedures deliver medication directly to the source of pain—blocking nerve signals, reducing inflammation, and providing relief that lasts for months.

  • Epidural steroid injections for targeted back and leg relief
  • Facet joint blocks and specialized radiofrequency ablation
  • Peripheral nerve blocks for chronic complex regional pain
Procedure Format Outpatient • 30–60 min

Medication Management & Therapy

Thoughtfully prescribed non-opioid regimens combined with physical therapy coordination and behavioral support address pain mechanisms for sustained functional improvement.

  • Non-opioid and opioid-sparing specialized pain protocols
  • Personalized physical therapy and rehabilitation planning
  • Behavioral and psychological support for long-term coping
Treatment Care Ongoing • Custom Plan
Conditions We Treat

Recognizing Common Pain Conditions

Untreated chronic pain affects mobility, sleep, and mental well-being. Our team identifies the root cause and builds a targeted treatment plan.

Chronic Back Pain

Persistent lower back or neck pain from disc degeneration, spinal stenosis, or muscle strain limiting daily activities.

Treatment: Epidural Injections

Neuropathic Pain

Burning, tingling, or shooting pain from nerve damage due to diabetes, shingles, or spinal nerve compression.

Treatment: Nerve Blocks

Arthritis & Joint Pain

Osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis causing stiffness, swelling, and chronic joint pain in knees and hips.

Treatment: Joint Injections

Headaches & Migraines

Recurrent migraines, tension headaches, and cluster headaches that disrupt work, sleep, and quality of life.

Treatment: Botox & Blocks
