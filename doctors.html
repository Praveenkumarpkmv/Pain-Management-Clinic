A Day in the Life at PainRelief

Our physicians don't just perform procedures; they immerse themselves in the diagnostic process and the long-term recovery of every patient. A typical day involves a blend of clinical consultations, complex interventional procedures, and multidisciplinary team rounds.

8a

Morning Rounds & Consults

Reviewing overnight patient reports and conducting new patient evaluations for complex pain syndromes.

11a

Interventional Suite

Performing image-guided procedures such as epidural injections and nerve blocks in our sterile suites.

2p

Multidisciplinary Rounds

Collaborating with physical therapists and psychologists to adjust comprehensive care plans.
